About Me

Hello! Aldison is an orangutan activist who is part of a dedicated team on a mission to protect nature and the incredible creatures that keep ecosystems balanced. His work focuses on saving orangutans and preserving their rainforest habitats through practical conservation efforts. Together with his team, Aldison supports rescue operations, habitat restoration, and community programs that help both wildlife and local people. The team is experienced in conservation work and committed to making a real difference in protecting endangered species. Aldison strongly believes that even small contributions matter. He often shares the idea that if many people each give just a small amount, it can add up to meaningful change—helping protect forests, save animals, and ensure these habitats continue to thrive. Through awareness campaigns and on-the-ground efforts, Aldison encourages people to see that supporting conservation is more than just donating—it’s about creating a lasting impact for orangutans and the forests they call home.
